## A flexible logo family

Choose the supplied composition that fits the available space.

### Primary signature

Use the mascot above the wordmark for hero applications. Add the tagline only where it remains comfortably readable.

### Compact signature

Use the horizontal composition in headers and shallow spaces. Omit the tagline when space is constrained.

### Independent elements

Use the mascot for icons and familiar navigation contexts. Use the wordmark alone where the context establishes RareIQ. The supplied Poppins-derived outlined wordmark is the v2 production adaptation; use its master instead of reconstructing it.

### Descriptors

## Give the mark room

Size for recognition first. Remove supporting detail before reducing the mark further.

### Clear space

Define x as one-quarter of visible mascot height, excluding transparent padding. Keep x clear around the complete signature. For a standalone wordmark, use half the capital I height.

### Digital starting limits

Minimum widths: stacked 180 px; horizontal 160 px; OCR horizontal 320 px; tagline signature 320 px. Use the full mascot at 48 px or larger. Select dedicated favicon exports for smaller placements and inspect at actual size.

### Physical reproduction

Start at 30 mm width for a signature, or 55 mm with tagline. Increase for absorbent paper, embroidery or difficult processes. Approve suitability through a physical proof.

## Control the surroundings

A clean surrounding field lets the mascot's color carry the expression.

### Dark and light

Use the light wordmark on Deep Space and the dark wordmark on Paper. Preserve the supplied mascot colors and keep its outline clearly separated.

### Photography

Choose an uncluttered area or solid brand panel. Keep faces, meaningful artwork and essential production information clear.

### Misuse

Do not stretch, rotate, crop, recolor parts, change eyes, replace lettering, or add outlines, shadows or glow. Avoid busy or equally saturated surrounding fields.

### One-color use

Use the supplied monochrome companion for restricted reproduction. It is an authorized adaptation, distinct from the rendered master. Confirm readability with the intended process.

## Color with intent

Quiet foundations support a vivid spectrum of creator energy.

### Foundations

Deep Space #080F1A is the primary dark field. Paper #F7F8FC is the light foundation. Let neutral surfaces dominate.

### Expression

Purple #A855F7, Pink #FF4EDB, Orange #FF9F43, Cyan #00E5FF and Mint #7CFFCB are supporting accents. Preserve the mascot’s supplied shading.

### Gradients

Use cyan–purple–pink for selected cool accents and purple–pink–orange for warm expressions. Keep gradients away from long text, dense tools and critical status information. Never recolor the mascot with them.

### Proportion

Begin with a mostly neutral layout, choose one dominant accent and use the remaining spectrum sparingly.

## Confident, readable type

Poppins provides the supporting typographic voice; the supplied wordmark remains artwork.

### Family and weights

Use the supplied Poppins Regular 400, Medium 500, Semibold 600 and Bold 700. Avoid synthetic weights. Provide system sans-serif fallbacks during loading.

### Reading rhythm

Start body copy at 16 px with approximately 1.5 line height. Aim for 45–75 characters per line and a consistent heading hierarchy.

### Tone

Prefer sentence case. Reserve tracked capitals for short editorial labels. Keep paragraphs left aligned and compact controls readable.

### Practical use

Keep ordinary text live where practical. Retain the supplied font license and confirm embedding permissions. Use the supplied outlined logo master; do not rebuild it with typed lettering.

## Clarity is a brand behavior

Expressive accents must coexist with understandable, usable production controls.

### Text and contrast

Test actual foreground/background pairs, including gradients and interaction states. No accent is automatically suitable for every text size or surface.

### Meaning

Assign functional success, warning, error and information roles. Pair color with labels or icons. Brand color alone must never imply verified readiness.

### Interaction

Preserve keyboard focus, semantic labels, adequate targets and useful disabled-state explanations. Keep important text outside image-only artwork and provide meaningful alternative text.

### Evaluation

Review dark/light themes at 1080p, 4K and narrow desktop widths. Check zoom, long labels and reduced motion before claiming accessibility conformance.

## Speak with calm confidence

Make the next useful action clear, and describe the evidence honestly.

### Voice

Use plain language, concrete verbs and short sentences. Be welcoming in introductions and precise in operations. Avoid effortless or flawless outcome promises.

### Brand examples

Intelligence for creators. Collectible intelligence within Producer, Please. Make room for your next discovery. These express positioning, not feature guarantees.

### Operational examples

Reading text… Check the extracted text before using it. Text recognition is unavailable. Use each message only when it describes the implemented state.

### Claims

Distinguish planned, experimental, locally tested and platform-verified capabilities. Avoid universal compatibility, guaranteed recognition or native broadcasting claims without evidence.

## Space creates distinction

Build a clear sequence: one message, one visual focus, one next action.

### Composition

Use a consistent grid, generous margins and an 8 px spacing rhythm. Establish reading order; group related content through alignment and proximity.

### Surfaces

Keep interface surfaces quiet. Use restrained depth and borders to clarify structure. Reserve large color fields for brand-led moments.

### Imagery

Show real creator contexts and truthful product captures. Preserve complete subjects and useful detail. Let abstract color support the message without obscuring the product.

### Format

Compose landscape and portrait separately. Preserve faces, logos and critical text. Label conceptual interfaces and illustrative applications when they could be mistaken for shipped functionality.

## Motion with a purpose

Movement should explain change and preserve the operator's attention.

### Brand motion

Prefer a short fade or small positional entrance of the complete signature. Preserve anatomy. Avoid distortion, flashing, indefinite bouncing and decoration during critical tasks.

### Reduced motion

Honor reduced-motion settings. Provide static equivalents and keep progress understandable without movement.

### OCR state language

Where implemented, distinguish ready, reading, review, confirmed, unavailable and failed. Detected text or confidence estimates do not establish verified identity or transactions.

### Honest feedback

Show measured progress only when supported; otherwise use a labeled working state. Preserve recovery actions. Avoid celebrating accuracy before review. These are design standards, not claims of implemented OCR states.

## Recognizable wherever it appears

Adapt the same identity to the context and the viewer's distance.

### App and avatar

Center the mascot on a clean field. Keep its face and accents within safe areas. Inspect square, rounded and circular crops at final size.

### Social and presentation

Pair one concise message with the signature. Reserve space for platform controls and framing. Preserve the brand hierarchy.

### Documents

Use a distinctive cover, quiet interior typography and consistent page furniture. Avoid repeating a large mascot on every page.

### Streaming

Compose landscape and portrait slates with clear safe zones. Keep permanent marks modest. Sample overlays do not demonstrate encoder integration, audio playback or destination receipt.

## A careful handoff

Choose the right file for the medium and preserve the provenance of the master.

### Digital files

Use PNG for rendered mascot artwork and SVG for genuine vector components. A bitmap embedded in SVG remains raster. Label that distinction in the inventory.

### Resolution

Choose sufficient native pixels for final placement. Upscaling does not restore detail. Inspect edges and eyes at actual output size.

### Print

Hex colors specify RGB. Saturated accents may shift in printing. Agree on the printer’s output profile and substrate, then approve a physical proof. Do not claim universal CMYK or spot-color matches.

### Package

Deliver book, sources, assets, tokens, font/license notes and inventory. Identify masters, provenance, production limitations and illustrative applications.

## Keep the identity coherent

Version the system, preserve its masters and keep every public claim tied to evidence.

### Ownership

The owner selected the colorful mascot direction. This v2 system defines its production adaptations and companion artwork. Signal Cut remains historical.

### Asset control

Select files through the inventory. Separate masters from exports and document material changes. New variants require a deliberate design decision; users should not reconstruct supplied masters.

### Product rollout

Package delivery differs from application integration. Update shared tokens, logos, manifests and identity checks together. Verify the running interface across supported themes and viewports.

### Release discipline

Record created, checked and untested layers. Preserve permissions and provenance. Public publishing, deployment and paid activity require separate authorization.
